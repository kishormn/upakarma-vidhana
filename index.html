Sri Vaishnava Sampradaya

Yajur Veda Upakarma Vidhana

The Annual Renewal of Vedic Study

Sātvika Tyāgam

"Bhagavānēva svaniyāmya svarūpa sthiti pravritti svashēshatā lēna svīyairatya kalyāṇa guṇagaṇaih... bhagavat-karmatām prapādayati."

Recite before and after every major step

Step I — Kāmokārshīt Japam

Chant 108 times to seek expiation for past lapses in regular Vedic study.

"Kāmokārshīt manyurakārshīt namō namah"

Step II — Brahma Yajna

If performing the full traditional sequence, face East and commence the offerings to the cosmic streams. If skipping, pass directly to Step III.

"Harih Ōm. Agnimmīḷē purōhitam yajñasya dēvamṛtvijam hotāram ratnad hātamam..."

Step III — Mahāsankalpam

Hold Akshata, fruit, and water in your right palm. Substitute your respective identity parameters in the underlined gaps below as you chant.

"Harih Ōm Tatsat... Shvētavarāha kalpē... samvatsarē, Uttarāyanē, Varsha ṛtau, Shrāvaṇa māsē, Shuklapakṣē, Pūrṇimāyām tithau, Shrāvaṇa nakṣatra yuktāyām...

[Insert Your Pravara & Gotra] gōtrōtpannah [Insert Your Name] sharmā aham...

...shrauta smārta vihita nitya karma anuṣṭhāna yōgyatā siddhyartham, nūtana yajñōpavīta dhāraṇam kariṣyē."

Step IV — Yajnopavīta Dhāraṇam

Place the new sacred thread loop over your right shoulder while reading the consecration verse.

"Yajñōpavītam paramam pavitram prajāpatēryatsahajam purastāt | Āyuṣyamagriyam pratimuñca shubhram jyōtiṣmatpamañca bālamastu ||"

Discard the old worn thread safely using the exit prayer:

"Upavītam bhinnatantu jīrṇam kaṣmāladūṣitam | Visṛjāmi yashōbāladīrghāyurastu mē ||"

Step V — Kāṇḍa Ṛṣi Tarpaṇam

Shift your thread to the necklace position (Nivēti) and offer holy water streams to the great seers of the Vedas.

— "Prājāpatyam kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

— "Sōmam kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

— "Agnim kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

— "Vishvān dēvān kāṇḍarṣīn tarpaṇāmi"
