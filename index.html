Acharya Dhyanam

Sātvika Tyāgam

"Bhagavānēva svaniyāmya svarūpa sthiti pravritti svashēshatā lēna svīyairatya kalyāṇa guṇagaṇaih bhagavad-ārādhanātmakam upākarmākhyam karma svasmai svaprītayē svayamēva kārayati."

Step I — Kāmokārshīt Japam

Chant 108 times using a Japa counter tracking sequence.

"Kāmokārshīt manyurakārshīt namō namah"

Step II — Mahāsankalpam

Hold Akshata, fruit, and gold coins in your right hand palm, covered by the left hand. Read the fully computed declaration aloud:

"Harih Ōm Tatsat... Śrī Govinda Govinda... Śrī Śvetavarāha kalpe, Vaivasvata manvantare, Kaliyuge, Prathama pāde, Jambūdvīpe, Bhārata varṣe, Bharata khaṇḍe, Śakoroh uttare pārśve...

Asmin vartamāne vyāvahārike prabhavādi ṣaṣṭhi saṁvatsarāṇāṁ madhye, Śarvari nāma saṁvatsare, Dakṣiṇāyane, Varṣa ṛtau, Siṁha māse, Śukla pakṣe, Paurṇamāsyāṁ śubha tithau, Vāsara yuktāyām, Śraviṣṭhā nakṣatra yuktāyām...

...

śarmā aham...

...mama śrauta smārta vihita nitya karma anuṣṭhāna yōgyatā siddhyartham, brahma-tejas-abhivṛddhyartham, nūtana yajñōpavīta dhāraṇam kariṣyē."

Step III — Yajnopavīta Dhāraṇam

Put on the fresh thread loop over your right hand index alignment shoulder while chanting:

"Yajñōpavītam paramam pavitram prajāpatēryatsahajam purastāt | Āyuṣyamagriyam pratimuñca shubhram jyōtiṣmatpamañca bālamastu ||"

Step IV — Kāṇḍa Ṛṣi Tarpaṇam

Shift thread loop position to Nivēti style and offer clear holy water mixed with sesame seed elements:

— "Prājāpatyam kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

— "Sōmam kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

— "Agnim kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

— "Vishvān dēvān kāṇḍarṣīn tarpaṇāmi"
