Iyengar Sampradaya

⚙️ Step 0: Personalize Your Ritual

Current Config: Bhārgava, Cyāvana, Āpnavāna, Aurva, Jāmadagnya pañcārṣeya prāvarānvita Śrīvatsa gōtrōtpannah

💡 Iyengar Nitya Rule: Sātvika Tyāgam

Perform this surrender prayer before and after every main step:
"Bhagavānēva svaniyāmya svarūpa sthiti pravritti svashēshatā lēna..."

Step 1

Kāmokārshīt Japam

Chant this expiatory mantra 108 times using visual count lines or a Japa mala.

"Kāmokārshīt manyurakārshīt namō namah"
Step 2 (Track A)

Brahma Yajna

Sit facing East. Offer respects to the Devas, Rishis, and Pitrus.

"Harih Ōm. Agnimmīḷē purōhitam yajñasya dēvamṛtvijam..."

⚠️ Remember to alter hand positions for Deva, Rishi, and Pitru Tarpanams.

Step 3

Mahāsankalpam

Hold Akshata, flowers, and water in your right palm. Read the customized text below.

"Harih Ōm Tatsat... Shvētavarāha kalpē... samvatsarē, Uttarāyanē, Varsha ṛtau, Shrāvaṇa māsē, Shuklapakṣē, Pūrṇimāyām tithau, Shrāvaṇa nakṣatra yuktāyām...

Bhārgava, Cyāvana, Āpnavāna, Aurva, Jāmadagnya pañcārṣeya prāvarānvita Śrīvatsa gōtrōtpannah [Your_Name] sharmā aham...

...shrauta smārta vihita nitya karma anuṣṭhāna yōgyatā siddhyartham, nūtana yajñōpavīta dhāraṇam kariṣyē."
Step 4

Yajnopavīta Dhāraṇam

Wear the new thread while reciting the consecration mantra.

"Yajñōpavītam paramam pavitram prajāpatēryatsahajam purastāt | Āyuṣyamagriyam pratimuñca shubhram jyōtiṣmatpamañca bālamastu ||"

Remove and discard the old thread with this mantra:

"Upavītam bhinnatantu jīrṇam kaṣmāladūṣitam | Visṛjāmi yashōbāladīrghāyurastu mē ||"
Step 5

Kāṇḍa Ṛṣi Tarpaṇam

Position thread like a necklace (Nivēti). Pour water mixed with Akshata/sesame seeds.

1. "Prājāpatyam kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

2. "Sōmam kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

3. "Agnim kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

4. "Vishvān dēvān kāṇḍarṣīn tarpaṇāmi"
