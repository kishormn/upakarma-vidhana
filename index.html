💡 Iyengar Anchor Rule: Sātvika Tyāgam

"Bhagavānēva svaniyāmya svarūpa sthiti pravritti svashēshatā lēna svīyairatya kalyāṇa guṇagaṇaih... bhagavat-karmatām prapādayati."

Step 1

Kāmokārshīt Japam

Chant this expiatory prayer 108 times to cleanse past lapses.

"Kāmokārshīt manyurakārshīt namō namah"
Step 2 (Track A)

Brahma Yajna

Face East. Pay systemic respects to Devas, Rishis, and Ancestors.

"Harih Ōm. Agnimmīḷē purōhitam yajñasya dēvamṛtvijam hotāram ratnad hātamam..."
Step 3

Mahāsankalpam

Hold offering items in your right hand. Read the custom compiled statement:

"Harih Ōm Tatsat... Shvētavarāha kalpē... samvatsarē, Uttarāyanē, Varsha ṛtau, Shrāvaṇa māsē, Shuklapakṣē, Pūrṇimāyām tithau, Shrāvaṇa nakṣatra yuktāyām...

Bhārgava, Cyāvana, Āpnavāna, Aurva, Jāmadagnya pañcārṣeya prāvarānvita Śrīvatsa gōtrōtpannah [Your Name] sharmā aham...

...shrauta smārta vihita nitya karma anuṣṭhāna yōgyatā siddhyartham, nūtana yajñōpavīta dhāraṇam kariṣyē."
Step 4

Yajnopavīta Dhāraṇam

Don the fresh thread loop while reciting:

"Yajñōpavītam paramam pavitram prajāpatēryatsahajam purastāt | Āyuṣyamagriyam pratimuñca shubhram jyōtiṣmatpamañca bālamastu ||"
Step 5

Kāṇḍa Ṛṣi Tarpaṇam

Set thread loop into necklace position (Nivēti) and pour clean water mix:

💧 "Prājāpatyam kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

💧 "Sōmam kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

💧 "Agnim kāṇḍarṣim tarpaṇāmi"

💧 "Vishvān dēvān kāṇḍarṣīn tarpaṇāmi"

Day 2 Morning

Gayatri Maha Japa Sankalpam

Concentrated chanting of the Gayatri mantra 1008 times.

"Harih Ōm Tatsat... Shvētavarāha kalpē... samvatsarē, Uttarāyanē, Varsha ṛtau, Shrāvaṇa māsē, Krishna/Shukla pakṣē...

Bhārgava, Cyāvana, Āpnavāna, Aurva, Jāmadagnya pañcārṣeya prāvarānvita Śrīvatsa gōtrōtpannah [Your Name] sharmā aham...

...chhandasām mātaram gāyatrīm āvāhayiṣyē... pāpanāśārtham aṣṭōttara sahasra (1008) saṅkhyayā gāyatrī mahā-mantra japam kariṣyē."
