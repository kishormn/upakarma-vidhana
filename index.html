Acharya Dhyanam

Initial Master Declaration — Saṅkalpam

Sit facing East. Change your current thread if structurally broken, place darbha grass on your lap, hold your palms layered together over your thigh, and recite this tailored temporal statement:

"Harih Ōm Tatsat... Śrī Govinda Govinda... Śrī Śvetavarāha kalpe, Vaivasvata manvantare, Kaliyuge, Prathama pāde, Jambūdvīpe, Bhārata varṣe, Bharata khaṇḍe, Śakoroh uttare pārśve, asmin vartamāne vyāvahārike prabhavādi ṣaṣṭhi saṁvatsarāṇāṁ madhye...

Parābhava nāma saṁvatsare, Dakṣiṇāyane, Varṣa ṛtau, māse, Śukla pakṣe, Paurṇamāsyāṁ śubha tithau, vāsera yuktāyām, nakṣatra yuktāyām, śubha yōga śubha karaṇa...

gōtrōtpannah śarmā aham...

...śrauta smārta vihita nitya karma anuṣṭhāna yōgyatā siddhyartham, nūtana yajñōpavīta dhāraṇa, kāmokārṣīt japa, brahma yajna, nava-kāṇḍa-ṛṣi tarpaṇam ca kramaśah kariṣyē."

I — Kāmokārshīt Japam

"Kāmokārshīt manyurakārshīt namō namah"

II — Brahma Yajna Tarpaṇam

"Harih Ōm. Agnimmīḷē purōhitam yajñasya dēvamṛtvijam hotāram ratnad hātamam... devān tarpaṇāmi, ṛṣīn tarpaṇāmi, pitṟn tarpaṇāmi."

III — Yajnopavīta Dhāraṇam

"Yajñōpavītam paramam pavitram prajāpatēryatsahajam purastāt | Āyuṣyamagriyam pratimuñca shubhram jyōtiṣmatpamañca bālamastu ||"
